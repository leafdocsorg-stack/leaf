Fill & sign

Complete the document, then sign it so nobody can change it unnoticed.

  1. Click the blank fields on the page and type your answers.
  2. Enter your name below.
  3. Press Seal and sign.
  4. Press Save at the top to keep your copy.

Sealing locks the current text and answers. Anyone can later check whether the document was changed after you signed. Demo signatures use a key created in your browser; a real signer would use a verified certificate.

Edit text

Click any text on the page and type, like in a word processor.

Tips
  • Select words and press B to make them bold.
  • Press Enter for a new paragraph or list item.
  • In a table, press Tab to move to the next cell.

Editing a signed document breaks its signature, which is how tampering is detected.

Press Save at the top when you are done.

Insert

Add something new. It goes after the block you last clicked, then opens for typing.
