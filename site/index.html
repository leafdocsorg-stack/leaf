Edit a PDF

Your file never leaves your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Open your PDFChoose it from your computer, or drop it on the page.
  2. Click and change itClick any text to retype it. Use the tools on the left to sign, highlight or fill in a form.
  3. SaveDownload the finished file when you are happy with it.

What you can do

Edit text

Click a line and type to change it.

Fill and sign

Fill in forms and draw or type your signature.

Mark up

Highlight, underline, draw, add notes and stamps.

Organise pages

Merge files, delete, rotate, reorder or split pages.

Black out and protect

Permanently hide private details, or add a password.

Scan to text

Make scanned pages searchable and copyable.
